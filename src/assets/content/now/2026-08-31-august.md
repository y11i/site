I started working at Valon. It feels like a culmination of the experiences I've accrued since I was 19 years old, and I'm happy as one could be as an employee here. 

One thing though, contributed most heavily to my satisfaction in the last 6 months, and it's that I've moved to San Francisco. The people you'll run into and meet, the old friends that will come out to visit, and the views of ocean kissed mountains over your shoulder on any random walk has made me swoon over this city. Dare I say better than Chicago?

I currently live in Duboce Triangle - a close distance to the oldest and largest darkroom in the United States that I haven't gotten the chance to take full advantage of yet. Somhow I only found out about it 2 months after moving here. There's a big embracement of analog photography and things that are whimsical and hippie all around. 

My life has never been so interesting, and it's only getting better.

Thinking about: Leveraging intuition / developing and defining my character archetype.
