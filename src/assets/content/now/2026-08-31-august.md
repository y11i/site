I started working at Valon. It feels like a culmination of my experiences since I was 19, and I find the work very engaging. 

I've been so happy with my decision to move to San Francisco. Meeting cool people, running into them randomly, old friends coming out to do things or even nothing just because, the views of ocean kissed mountains over your shoulder on a casual walk after work... It's all made me swoon over this city. Chicago might still be end game, but I'm getting used to SF. 

I'm living in Duboce Triangle next to the oldest and largest darkroom in the United States. I haven't gotten the chance to take full advantage of it yet and somehow only found out about it 2 months after moving here. There's a big embracement of analog photography and things that are whimsical and hippie. 

My life has never been so interesting, and I keep doing things that seemed out of reach when I was younger.

Thinking about: Leveraging gut feelings and intuition / developing and defining my character archetype.

Reading: On the Road by Jack Kerouac (also craving to be back on the road again)
